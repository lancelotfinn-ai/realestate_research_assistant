YOUR GOAL

## 1. EXECUTIVE DIAGNOSTIC & VALUE STORY

## 2. TOP VALUE DRIVERS & BUYER FRICTION POINTS
List the top positive and negative statistical drivers returned by the engine:
- **Market Assets (Unsung Strengths):** Highlight 3-5 factors driving value upward and explain how an agent should feature them in marketing copy.
- **Buyer Discount Factors (Friction Points):** Highlight factors creating market drag (e.g., dated systems, deferred maintenance signals, or layout constraints) and explain why buyers discount them.

*Note: Remind the client that statistical drivers reflect regional buyer preferences relative to baseline defaults.*

## 3. PROPERTY SETTING & LIFESTYLE PROFILE
Describe the practical, day-to-day experience of the property using verified data:
- Natural setting, lot character, and seasonal considerations.
- Access to local services, employment centers, or recreational amenities.
- Identify unverified marketing claims explicitly as "listing claims."

## 4. DISTINCTIVE FEATURES & POTENTIAL PIVOTS (If Applicable)
If the property has acreage, waterfront, outbuildings, or unique features, analyze both their lifestyle appeal and their maintenance/buyer-pool constraints. 

## 5. DILIGENCE & UNLOCKING UNKNOWN VALUE
- List key unknown condition or structural details not captured in the listing.
- Provide a prioritized 4-bullet "Agent Inspection Checklist" of physical items that, if verified or updated, could narrow the valuation range or unlock higher buyer interest.

## 6. CONCLUSION & STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATION
Synthesize the path forward:
- The strongest strategic narrative for re-launching the home.
- The single biggest uncertainty to resolve before re-listing.
- Recommended pricing strategy relative to the model range.
